--- Slide 6: Combining three pillars of tech into a single competition. ---

Notes:
  Tech-Triathlon exists because real products are never built by one discipline alone.
  Most competitions ask you to pick a lane. You either design, or you build, or you do the data science. We combine all three into a single competition, because that is how a product actually comes together. UX design, engineering, and artificial intelligence.
  You will be tested on all three.

--- Slide 7: Theme of the ---
Tech-Triathlon 2026
The Intelligent
Enterprise

Notes:
  The theme for Tech-Triathlon 2026 is The Intelligent Enterprise.
  Every business has information. The challenge is putting it to work. An intelligent enterprise connects its people, processes and systems so it can make better decisions, cut out unnecessary work, and respond faster when things change.
  This answers questions like How does a business capture the right information? How does work move between teams? How quickly can it spot a problem and respond to it?
  Becoming an intelligent enterprise means improving how those everyday things happen. Not one big transformation, but one solution at a time, each one part of a bigger journey toward a business that works better.
  This year, your team takes on one part of that journey.

--- Slide 9: Unfolding the Triathlon ---
The Phases

Notes:
  Now, the part that has changed this year, and it is the biggest change we have made.

--- Slide 36: Rules & Regulations ---
01
Deadline
The submission form will close after the deadline.
02
Model Restrictions
You are restricted from using any pre-trained models, except for any data generation or pre-processing.
03
API Usage
Proprietary API-based services for data preprocessing are prohibited and will affect scoring.
04
No-Code & AI Tools
Usage of Low-code/No-code AI tools or fully automated end-to-end modelling tools are restricted.

Notes:
  The main ground rules are that submissions forms will close after the deadline. You are restricted from using any pre-trained models except for any data generation or pre-processing in which case you need to include the reason behind it and how chose that model.
  Proprietary API based service for data preprocessing are prohibited and will affect scoring.
  The focus of datathon is to enable participants to build and train their models using code. This provides insights into their thinking pattern and shows understanding behind the model architectures. Therefore, usage of low-code or no code AI tools or fully automated end-to-end modelling tools are restricted.

02

Notes:

Model and architecture implementation is evaluated by looking at the model used, it’s applicability, the architecture design, and the reasoning behind the model choice, performance score evaluates the goodness of the model, out of box thinking is where we assess the creativity of the solution and finally the demo video where you explain the solution and the challenges you faced.
