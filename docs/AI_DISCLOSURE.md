---

## What Was AI-Assisted

1. **HTML/CSS prototype code generation.** The 30 high-fidelity HTML screens were generated with AI assistance using detailed prompts grounded in our manually-written specifications (COMPREHENSIVE_SITEMAP_NEW.md, ui_ux_guidelines.md, instructions.md). The AI translated wireframe specifications and design tokens into working HTML/CSS.

2. **Design token CSS file.** The `material-tokens.css` file containing M3 CSS custom properties was generated with AI assistance based on our manually-defined color palette and typography decisions.

3. **Documentation drafting.** Supporting documents (personas, style guide, degradation rationales) were drafted with AI assistance based on team-written source materials and the Challenge Booklet requirements.

---

## What Was NOT AI-Assisted

1. **Product thinking and scope decisions.** The decision of which screens to build, which features to include, and which to deliberately exclude was made entirely by the team. The 6-stage workflow mapping, the choice of 3 degradation scenarios, and the restraint decisions (no chat, no payroll, no inventory ERP) were human decisions.

2. **User persona research.** The environmental details, pain points, and working conditions for each persona were derived from the team's analysis of the Challenge Booklet and Kick-off Session materials, not generated by AI.

3. **Design system selection.** The decision to use Material Design 3, the persona-based theme strategy, and the accessibility standards (64px touch targets, no pure red, trilingual buffers) were human design decisions made before any AI code generation began.

4. **Information architecture.** The screen flow connections (e.g., dispatcher deferral → loader revision alert, driver delivery → store receipt) were mapped manually based on the operational workflow in the Challenge Booklet.

5. **Wireframe specifications.** The detailed wireframe layouts in COMPREHENSIVE_SITEMAP_NEW.md were written by the team and served as the specification that guided AI-assisted code generation.

---

## How AI Was Used

Our approach was **specification-first, AI-assisted execution:**

1. The team analyzed the Challenge Booklet and defined the product scope, personas, and screen inventory manually.
2. Detailed wireframe specifications and design guidelines were written as markdown documents.
3. These specifications were provided to AI tools as prompts to generate the HTML/CSS prototype code.
4. Every generated screen was reviewed against the specifications and iteratively refined through multiple rounds of feedback.
5. Documentation was drafted with AI assistance and reviewed/edited by the team.

The AI served as an implementation accelerator — translating human-authored design decisions into working code — not as the creative or strategic decision-maker.
